Phase 1 · Unit economics

Daily, then monthly, then six months

07

Four passengers pay ₹600. That trip is ₹2,400. The return is another four, so the day is ₹4,800. CNG and toll take ₹2,800. Thirty days leave ₹60,000 before any CEO salary. Six months of fares are ₹8.64 lakh. Six months of CNG and toll are ₹5.04 lakh. Operating profit is ₹3.60 lakh.

09 · Partnership

Build the business, not only the cars

18

I am not asking partners to buy all the cars or invest everything from day one.

I will take responsibility for the first car and prove the first stage. When that stage works, we move together into the second car, then the third, using real demand, cost, and profit data.

Let us take the risk together today, build the foundation together, and enjoy the results together in the future.

01 · ProveSupport the Car 1 launch

The founder takes the first car and the first six months.

02 · LearnHelp fill the seats

Passenger acquisition and repeat travel on the live route.

03 · GrowCar 2 after the proof

Vijayawada opens only when Khammam’s numbers are real.

04 · ReinvestCar 3 on Khammam

Density on the route that has already been proved.

05 · ExpandBuild for the long term

Shared ownership of the network, car by car.

About ROVEYA

Comfort has a seat of its own.

22

Founder and Chief Executive

Shaik Asif Nawaz

ROVEYA · Hyderabad

ROVEYA is built for passengers who want a calm journey. You get a comfortable seat that belongs to you, in a clean cabin, with room left on purpose.

Care that travels with you. A comfortable seat of your own, mineral water, a planned break, and luggage attended to before you sit.

Your own seatOne passenger

Every seat is given to one passenger. Two people are never asked to share one place.

A clean seatPrepared before you sit

Seats are cleaned and prepared before you sit. Hygiene is part of the ride, not an extra.

Room we keep open7 seats, 4 or 5 passengers

The car can hold seven. We stop at four or five so the cabin never feels packed.

Mineral waterKept ready

A sealed mineral water bottle is kept for you, ready when you want it.

A planned breakOne stop, same seat

On a longer journey we make one planned stop. You return to the same seat.

LuggagePlaced before you board

Luggage is collected and placed before you board, clear of the next passenger.
