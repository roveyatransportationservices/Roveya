01

What Phase 1 is

Phase 1 is the working public company. A person can read ROVEYA, write to the desk, and a passenger already in the vehicle can pay for the seat. Staff can see that work from the operations desk.

Public websiteHome, About, Services, Pool a Ride, and Contact. The story is one seat, a clean cabin, and four or five passengers in a 7-seater.
Seat paymentThe mark in the vehicle opens the pay screen. The passenger pays cash or online. The fare is seats times the fare of that destination.
DeskRides, destinations, customers, messages, testimonials, the vehicle mark, and the desk settings live in one staff screen.
RecordNames, rides, payments, contact notes, and published comments are stored. A visit is written into the activity log.

02

Architecture

The codebase is one application. The page the person sees, the check behind it, and the store sit in layers. A layer speaks only to the one beneath it.

1. Pages. Home, About, Services, Pool a Ride, Contact, Pay, and the desk. These draw the screen. They do not calculate a fare or talk to the bank.
2. Routes. Each action has an address: create a ride, send a message, open an online order, confirm cash, publish a comment. The page calls the address. The address checks the request.
3. Rules. Seat fare is fare per seat times the number of seats, from 1 to 8. A ride moves through created, payment waiting, cash waiting, paid, failed, or cancelled. Staff are CEO or Admin.
4. Store. Supabase holds customers, destinations, rides, payments, messages, comments, and staff. Razorpay takes the online payment. The activity log records a visit.

03

Content of the public site

Every public page says the same thing in a different room. The seat is allocated to one passenger. The cabin is cleaned. A 7-seater carries four or five. Water, one planned break, and luggage are part of the ride.

PageWhat the reader leaves with
HomeThe promise, six services, six reasons, a moving row of comments, and the desk form.
AboutOne seat, a clean seat, room left open, and a plan of the 7-seater.
ServicesMineral water, one planned break that is not skipped, luggage attended to, and the fare for that seat.
Pool a RideA road has a start, a destination, and a time. Each passenger has their own seat.
ContactPhone, WhatsApp, email, and a form. After it is saved, the page confirms that the desk will call.
PayName, optional mobile, seats, destination, then cash or pay online.

Comments

Published comments move in a row, three across on a wide screen. The row pauses when the pointer rests on it. The three comments already stored stay. Further comments were added so the row has enough to move.

WhatsApp on the home page, the contact page, and the footer opens with a ready note about a seat, a route, or a ride already moving.

  1. The passenger enters a name. A mobile number is optional.
  2. They choose how many seats, from 1 to 8, and the destination.
  3. The total is the destination fare times the seats. That amount is shown before they pay.
  4. The screen states that the fare is for their own clean seat, that mineral water is included, and that luggage is attended to before they sit.
  5. They choose cash or pay online. There is no separate Google Pay or PhonePe button.
  6. After payment, a short note can be left. Feedback is accepted once the ride is paid.
06

Cash and online payment

CashThe ride is stored as cash waiting. The desk confirms it. Until that confirmation, the ride is not paid.
OnlineAn order is opened with Razorpay. The passenger pays in that window. The signature is checked, then the ride is marked paid.

Razorpay also sends a notice to the site when a payment is captured. That address accepts a post only. Opening it in a browser returns “method not allowed”, which is the correct answer.

Phase 1 online payment is in test mode. A live bank key is used only when the founder supplies it. The test window is enough to prove the path.

The amount stored on the ride is a snapshot: fare per seat times seats. Later edits to a destination fare do not rewrite a ride already created.

09

Colour and type

The site and this document use one palette. Night is the ground. Cream is the page. Burgundy is the action. Gold marks a rule or a live point.

Cream#F6F1DCWords on night, and the paper of this document
Gold#D6A000Lines, stars, focus

Form fields stay night with cream writing, including after the browser fills them in. A light form, such as a search on cream, keeps dark ink on cream so the field does not turn white against the template.

The mark is a cream R on night, with a gold edge and a gold underline. Display lines use a serif. Labels and buttons use a plain sans.

How to read the profit plan beside this

The five-year profit plan is a separate paper. It prices Car 1 on Hyderabad–Khammam with the founder driving, and Car 2 on Hyderabad–Vijayawada with two drivers. This website does not calculate that yearly profit. It collects the seat fare the desk has set for each destination.

Car 1 in that plan has no hired driver. The figure kept for it is ₹60,000 a month before EMI, insurance, servicing, and the office.
